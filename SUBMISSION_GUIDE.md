## Single continuous video plan

Record the phone and website in one uninterrupted take. Keep both bags visible when demonstrating synchronization and do not refresh either client.

1. Show the production website URL and open the installed **LUMA Beauty** Android app.
2. Open **Account** on both clients and show that the same customer account is signed in. If starting signed out, sign in on web and mobile with the same email/password account. Keep the password hidden while typing.
3. On the website, add a product to the bag.
4. Move immediately to the mobile app and show that the product appears automatically.
5. In the mobile app, increase the quantity or add a second product.
6. Return immediately to the website and show that the bag updates automatically without reloading the page.
7. Remove an item from mobile and show the removal reflected on web, again without refreshing.
8. Briefly show the mobile checkout screen and the account's live-sync message.
9. End by showing the website URL and the installed app name together.

## Recording checks

- Use a real installed APK, not the Expo Go preview.
- Record a single continuous video with no cuts.
- Keep the browser address bar visible when showing the website.
- Make the cart quantity and product names readable.
- Do not manually refresh during the synchronization demonstration.
- Avoid showing passwords, private email inboxes, API keys, or dashboards.
- Play the finished recording once before submission and confirm that login and both sync directions are clearly visible.
